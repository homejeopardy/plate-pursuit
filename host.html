Plate list

One plate per line: PLATE | answer. Add | x2 at the end for double points (or click 2× in the plate list). To colour-match the plate to the answer when it's revealed, add the pieces in order: L8 AGAIN | Late again | L8=Late, AGAIN=again. Pieces that are the same get the same colour. A clue shown under the question: | hint: On a cow-print car. A line starting with ## sets the question shown above the plates that follow it (e.g. ## Who drives this car?), for plates that ask a different question. Saving starts the plates over; scores are kept.

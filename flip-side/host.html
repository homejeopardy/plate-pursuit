Game

Board: 20 cards. Seven pairs of points (100 to 700), a TAKE pair (steals the biggest card another team matched this puzzle), two LOSE A TURN cards (turning one over ends your turn, and that card comes off) and two WILD cards (a wild matches any card, and that card's twin comes off too). Only the team that solves banks its matches, plus the solve bonus. If the board is cleared without a solve, every team can buzz in.
Final wager puzzle ★ (Micronesia to start with; mark others with ★ in the Puzzles list): each team bets up to its score, or up to 500 if it has less. Then the board plays as usual, but any team can buzz in at any point. A right answer wins the wager and a wrong one loses it, and that team sits out the rest of the puzzle. Card points don't count. Next puzzle skips final wager puzzles.
